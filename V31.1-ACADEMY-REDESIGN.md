# V31.1 — Academy UI Redesign

- 68 published curriculum lessons remain in the migration.
- Learning navigation redesigned into 4 tracks with nested modules instead of a long horizontal category rail.
- Catalog filters remain compact and secondary.
- Lesson cards redesigned with clearer hierarchy and metadata.
- Lesson modal redesigned as a premium learning page with hero, video, theory, facts, practice, quiz and notes.
- Quiz is now one-question-at-a-time to avoid taking most of the page.
- Supports single-select, true/false and multi-select question types.
- After checking, correct answers are highlighted and selected wrong answers are marked separately; per-option feedback is shown.
- Hints are collapsible.
- Quiz progress and score are visible without a long form.
